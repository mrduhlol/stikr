05 / Your photos stay yours

Your library.
Your choice.

stikr processes photos locally. Save local backup files when you want to keep a copy. No analytics or telemetry.

Photos and packs stay local

You choose photos with Android's system photo picker. stikr does not ask for storage permission. The app processes the selected photo on device and saves packs as files in its private storage.

There is no stikr server receiving your photos. No sign-in or watermark is required.

The cut-out model

Automatic cut-out uses ML Kit subject segmentation on your phone. Google Play services fetches the model on first use. That model download needs a connection; photo processing happens locally.

Cut-out requires Google Play services. If it is unavailable, stikr keeps the full photo. Sticker creation, editing and sharing do not require a Stikr account; Local backup needs no account or connection.

Local backup files

The folder icon opens Save backup file and Restore from file. A backup ZIP contains packs, stickers, private source photos, edit settings and thumbnails. Android's file picker lets you save it in Downloads or another folder on your device. Stikr does not upload backups or connect an account.

Backups are manual snapshots. Save a new one after changes, and wait for confirmation before uninstalling. After reinstalling, choose Restore from file and select your ZIP. Existing local packs are preserved; matching packs use the newer version and local data wins ties. Android automatic app backup is disabled.

You choose when to share

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, stikr provides your chosen sticker pack to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business through Android's sticker-pack contract.

When you choose Share, Android's share sheet sends a PNG to the app you select. These actions share content at your request. The destination app handles what happens next.

About this site
