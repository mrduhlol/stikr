05 / Your photos stay yours

Your library.
Your choice.

stikr processes photos locally. Connect Google only if you want cloud backup. No analytics or telemetry.

Photos and packs stay local

You choose photos with Android's system photo picker. stikr does not ask for storage permission. The app processes the selected photo on device and saves packs as files in its private storage.

There is no stikr server receiving your photos. No sign-in or watermark is required.

The cut-out model

Automatic cut-out uses ML Kit subject segmentation on your phone. Google Play services fetches the model on first use. That model download needs a connection; photo processing happens locally.

Cut-out requires Google Play services. If it is unavailable, stikr keeps the full photo. Sticker creation, editing and sharing do not require a Stikr account; Google backup needs a connection.

Optional Google backup

The profile icon offers Google connection. After connection, Back up now saves packs, sticker images, private source photos, editor settings and thumbnails in your Google Drive application-data folder. Automatic backup is off until you enable it and runs while the app is open. No Stikr server receives this archive.

The app requests Drive application-data access and your verified email to identify the connected account. Access tokens stay in memory; the account label and last successful backup time are stored locally. Disconnect clears the local connection and requests revocation, but does not delete your backup or local packs. Delete application data through Google Drive settings if you want to remove the stored archive.

After reinstalling, connect the same account and choose Restore. Without a completed backup, uninstalling can remove your library. Google project setup and limitations.

You choose when to share

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, stikr provides your chosen sticker pack to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business through Android's sticker-pack contract.

When you choose Share, Android's share sheet sends a PNG to the app you select. These actions share content at your request. The destination app handles what happens next.

About this site

This documentation is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no JavaScript, analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking pixels. Its images and Anton font are served with the site.
