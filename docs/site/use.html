02 / From photo to pack

Make it a sticker.

Start with a photo on your phone. Finish with a sticker you can send.

Make your first sticker

  1. Open stikr and tap Make a sticker. You can also create a pack first.
  2. Pick a photo with Android's system photo picker. stikr cuts out the subject automatically when cut-out is available.
  3. Adjust the outline thickness and choose white or ink.
  4. For a caption, enter your text, choose cream, ink, butter, or cherry, and place it at the top or bottom.
  5. Tap Add to “pack name” to save the sticker.

If automatic cut-out is unavailable, the full photo still works. Use the cut-out toggle to keep or remove the background when available.

Send a pack to WhatsApp

  1. Add at least 3 stickers to a pack. A WhatsApp pack holds 3–30 stickers.
  2. Open the pack and tap Add to WhatsApp.
  3. Complete the import in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

If WhatsApp is unavailable, share a sticker as an image instead.

Share one sticker

Tap the sticker in its pack and choose Share. Pick the destination in Android's share sheet. stikr shares a PNG image.

Keep your packs organised

Create, rename, or delete packs, and add or delete stickers inside them. Packs live in the app's private storage on your phone.

stikr makes static stickers. Animated stickers, a tray-icon picker, multiple subjects, extra caption fonts, and emoji suggestions are not shipped.
